Planning for pregnancy is the first step toward giving your baby the best start in life. **Pre-conceptional counselling** helps you understand and optimize your health before you conceive — ensuring a smoother pregnancy and a healthier outcome for both mother and baby.

During your visit, we focus on:

* Reviewing your medical, surgical, and family history

* Checking and managing conditions like diabetes, thyroid disorders, or anemia

* Ensuring vaccinations are up to date

* Discussing nutrition, folic acid, and lifestyle habits

* Guidance on timing conception and fertility awareness

By addressing potential risks early, you can enter pregnancy **well-prepared, confident, and healthy**.
