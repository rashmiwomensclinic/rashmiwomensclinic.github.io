**Cryosurgery** is a simple, safe, and minimally invasive procedure used to treat abnormal cells on the cervix or other minor gynaecological conditions. It works by freezing the affected tissue, allowing healthy cells to grow back naturally.

At our clinic, cryosurgery is performed as an **outpatient procedure** — no stitches, no hospital stay, and minimal discomfort. It is commonly recommended for:

* Cervical erosion (ectopy)

* Mild cervical dysplasia (abnormal Pap smear results)

* Chronic cervicitis or recurrent vaginal discharge

The procedure takes only a few minutes and offers excellent results with quick recovery.
