**Hysteroscopy** is a minimally invasive procedure that allows our specialists to **examine the inside of the uterus** and treat certain conditions without major surgery. It is a safe and effective way to diagnose and manage various gynaecological issues.

Our hysteroscopy services help with:

* Abnormal uterine bleeding

* Uterine fibroids or polyps

* Infertility evaluation

* Retained products of conception

* Congenital uterine abnormalities

Performed on an outpatient basis, hysteroscopy offers **quick recovery, minimal discomfort, and precise treatment**.
