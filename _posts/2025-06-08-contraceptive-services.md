Family planning is an important part of women’s health. Choosing the right **contraceptive method** helps you plan your family, space pregnancies safely, and take control of your reproductive health.

At our clinic, we offer **personalized contraceptive counselling** to help you select a method that best suits your lifestyle, health, and future plans. Our services include:

* Temporary methods like pills, injections, intrauterine devices (IUDs), and condoms

* Permanent options such as sterilization procedures

* Guidance on natural family planning and fertility awareness

* Counseling for newly married couples and postpartum mothers

Our goal is to help you make **safe, comfortable, and confident choices** about your reproductive health.
