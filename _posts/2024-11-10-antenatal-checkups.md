Regular **antenatal checkups** are an essential part of a healthy pregnancy. These visits help monitor both the mother’s and baby’s well-being, detect any potential complications early, and provide guidance for a smooth pregnancy journey.

During your antenatal visits, we:

* Track your baby’s growth and heartbeat

* Monitor your blood pressure, weight, and general health

* Screen for conditions like anemia, diabetes, or high blood pressure

* Offer nutritional and lifestyle advice

* Discuss birth planning and answer your questions

Early and consistent care makes a big difference — helping you feel confident, informed, and supported every step of the way.
