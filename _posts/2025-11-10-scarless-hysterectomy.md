For many women, the idea of surgery can be worrying—especially when it involves visible scars or a long recovery. The good news is that with advances in minimally invasive surgery, **a hysterectomy can now often be done without any visible scars**.

At **Rashmi Women’s and Multi-Speciality Clinic**, we offer **scarless hysterectomy**, also known as Non descent vaginal hysterectomy. This  technique allows the uterus to be removed through the natural vaginal opening.

That means:

* **No abdominal cuts or visible scars**

* **Less pain and faster recovery**

* **Shorter hospital stay**

* **Quicker return to daily life**

This approach is just as safe and effective as traditional methods, and it’s suitable for many women who need a hysterectomy for conditions such as fibroids, heavy bleeding.

Our team takes time to explain every step, answer your questions, and ensure you feel supported before, during, and after surgery. We understand that surgery is a big decision—and we’re here to make it as smooth and stress-free as possible.
