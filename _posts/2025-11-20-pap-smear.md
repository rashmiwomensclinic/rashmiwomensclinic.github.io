A **Pap smear** is a simple and quick test that helps detect abnormal cells in the cervix before they develop into serious problems, including cervical cancer. Regular screening is an essential part of preventive women’s health care.

Pap smear is universally recommended for all women and helps you:

* Detect precancerous changes early

* Monitor cervical health regularly

* Take timely action if any abnormalities are found

* Maintain peace of mind about your reproductive health

The procedure is **painless, safe, and quick**, usually done during a routine checkup.
