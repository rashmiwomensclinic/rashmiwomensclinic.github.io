Many women experience menstrual or gynaecological issues at some point in their lives — from irregular or heavy periods to pelvic pain, infections, or hormonal imbalances. With the right evaluation and care, these problems can be effectively managed to restore comfort and confidence.

At our clinic, we provide comprehensive care for a wide range of women’s health concerns, including:

* Irregular, painful, or heavy menstrual cycles

* Polycystic ovarian syndrome (PCOS) and hormonal disorders

* Fibroids, endometriosis, and ovarian cysts

* Vaginal infections and discharge

* Pelvic pain and urinary issues

* Premenstrual syndrome (PMS) and menopausal problems

* LNG-IUS insertion in indicated cases

Our approach focuses on **accurate diagnosis, individualized treatment, and long-term wellness**, using both medical and minimally invasive options where appropriate.
